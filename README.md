# Employee Attrition Prediction & Retention Dashboard

## 📊 Project Overview

The Employee Attrition Prediction & Retention Dashboard is an interactive HR analytics project developed using Microsoft Power BI. The dashboard analyzes employee data to identify attrition patterns, high-risk employees, workforce trends, and factors associated with employee turnover.

The project helps HR teams understand employee attrition and use data-driven insights to support employee retention and workforce planning.

## 🎯 Objectives

- Analyze overall employee attrition trends.
- Calculate and monitor the employee attrition rate.
- Identify departments with higher attrition.
- Analyze attrition based on gender and tenure.
- Examine the relationship between performance and attrition.
- Analyze training hours based on attrition status.
- Identify employees with higher risk scores.
- Support HR decision-making and retention planning.

## 📌 Key KPIs

- Total Employees
- Attrition Rate
- Average Tenure
- Overtime Employees
- Average Performance Rating
- High-Risk Employees

## 📈 Dashboard Analysis

### Attrition by Department
Compares employee attrition rates across different departments such as Sales, IT, HR, Operations, Marketing, and Finance.

### Attrition by Gender
Shows the distribution of employees and attrition patterns across male and female employees.

### Attrition by Tenure Group
Analyzes attrition according to employee tenure groups:
- 0–2 Years
- 3–5 Years
- 6–10 Years
- 10+ Years

### Training Hours by Attrition Status
Compares average training hours between employees who stayed and employees who left.

### Performance Rating vs Attrition
Analyzes employee performance ratings in relation to attrition status.

### Top 10 High-Risk Employees
Displays employees with higher risk scores along with their department, tenure, risk score, and attrition status.
